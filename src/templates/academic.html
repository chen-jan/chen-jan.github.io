I’m a fourth-year BSc Information Systems student at the Technical University of Munich and a student at the Center for Digital Technology and Management (CDTM).

I love building AI systems across language, time series, voice, and music. Currently, I’m especially curious about what large generative models learn internally, and how this interpretability can help with alignment, and safety topics.

The past few years have taken me through startups, consulting, and AI research in Singapore and the US. Outside university, I’ve organized scholarship information sessions across Germany, led the TU Investment Club, and tried to find my way back to the piano whenever I can.
