Stipendieninfoevents

In 2023, a friend and I realised that access to scholarship information depended far too much on where you went to school. After visiting schools to talk about our own scholarships, we started recruiting volunteers and emailing every high school in our state.

Our first sessions were on Zoom: a chance for students to ask scholarship recipients their questions directly. Today, we work with scholars from all 13 of Germany’s federally funded scholarship foundations, with a particular focus on rural regions; more than 80 volunteers have helped us reach over 5,000 students and teachers from over 600 schools.
